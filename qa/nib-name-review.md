# The name: does "Nib" hold up?

*2026-10-07 · open question 14 (PRD §10) · a first screen from public web searches: what's already
called each name, and a look for US trademarks in software. It isn't a trademark clearance; a
search of the USPTO's own records and a lawyer's opinion come before sale.*

**In short:** Nib is taken in its own category. Two notes apps for the Mac are called Nib in 2026,
one of them a quick-capture window behind a global shortcut, sold as a notarized download from
`nib.palu.io`. Selling as Nib means arriving third, under the same name, in the same category.
**Rename before sale.** Of the names checked, **Spool** is the clearest, then **Stet** and
**Tittle**; the rest are taken by notes or writing apps.

## Nib

**Apps already called Nib:**
- **Nib, by Palú Labs** ([palu.io/nib](https://palu.io/nib)): a small notes app for the Mac. A
  global shortcut (⌃⇧Space) brings a window up over whatever you're doing, and each note is a
  plain Markdown file on the Mac. It's a notarized direct download (about 3 MB, macOS 26), © 2026.
  That's the job of Nib's scratch window, sold the way Nib will be, from a domain shaped like
  Nib's planned one (`nib.palu.io`, `nib.banast.as`).
- **Nib, by FIVENINE DESIGN** ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/nib-2)): notes
  for iPhone, iPad and Mac, with on-device transcription and a Markdown editor, launched in 2026.
- **nib!** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/nib/id6749094777)): brushes for illustration
  apps, on iPhone. Unrelated.

**Other users of the word,** all in the way of a search for the app:
- the health insurer nib (its member app, "my nib", came first in a search for "nib app");
- banks' apps (NIB Mobile, NIBtera);
- Apple's `.nib` Interface Builder files and `NSNib` (every "nib" search for the Mac);
- [pynib](https://pypi.org/project/pynib/), a Python framework for Mac menu bar apps.

**Trademarks:** a US "NIB" mark in class 9, the class for downloadable software, is on
[Justia](https://trademarks.justia.com/751/71/nib-75171492.html) (serial 75171492; its owner and
status aren't in the screen). Apart from any registration, Palú Labs' and FIVENINE's use of Nib on
the same kind of goods gives them priority where they began before Nib's sale.

promise ("WRITE IT DOWN. LOSE NOTHING_"). Each was searched as an app (above all a Mac notes or text
app) and, for the three that passed, as a software trademark.

## Recommendation

**Spool,** if a name from outside the pen is fine: it's the only one checked with no Mac app under
it (an iPhone photo app is the nearest), and it pairs with Blit as two old words for moving bytes about. The icon and the menu bar mark
would need new pixel art (a spool of thread, or tape on a reel). **Stet** has the best meaning but
a Mac text tool launched under it today; **Tittle** keeps the PRD's "smallest part" idea and sits
near a golf brand. Before choosing, search each finalist in the
[USPTO's trademark search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) (classes 9 and 42) and with a lawyer.

## Rune (the user's pick, 2026-10-07)

From a second brainstorm, for a name with hacker roots that puts raw text first. **Rune** is Plan 9's
word for a single character: Rob Pike and Ken Thompson made UTF-8 for Plan 9, and Go still calls a
character a rune. Blit was Pike's terminal at Bell Labs (1982), named for the bitblt operation, so
the two names come from the same place; and runes are letters cut to last, as the text here is.

**Already there:**
- **Rune, a text editor** on [SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/projects/rune/): Java, GPL 3, for
  Mac, Windows and Linux; last updated 2022-12-11, no downloads this week. Dormant.
- **Sticky Runes** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6769976786)): a $0.99 Mac menu bar
  canvas of sticky notes (2026). Another name, close in word and kind.
- **iRuneText 2 and RunePDF** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id415946564)), from the
  developer Runecats: a simple Mac text editor and a PDF editor with "Rune" inside their names.
- **Rune, a scripting language for Rust** ([docs.rs](https://docs.rs/rune)): what a coder's search
  for "rune" finds first. Its `rune-cli` installs a `rune` command, which a `rune` for this app would
  share a name with on the PATH of anyone who has both.
- **The App Store:** mostly rune-reading apps and games (Rune Magic, Way of the Runes, Rune Mage).

**Trademarks:** no US RUNE mark in class 9 turned up; RUUNE, AI speech recognition offered online
(class 42), lists RUNE as its pseudo mark ([Justia](https://trademarks.justia.com/997/46/ruune-99746007.html)).
**Roon**, the music software, is said exactly as "rune", and both are downloadable software:
likelihood of confusion weighs sound as well as spelling, so the goods being far apart (music
playback, a text editor) is the case to put to the lawyer.

**Against Nib's screen:** no current Mac notes or text app under the exact name, where Nib has two
from 2026. Search is noisier among coders (the Rust language) and on the App Store (divination),
quieter than Nib's (Apple's `.nib`, the insurer). Before sale: the USPTO's search for RUNE in
classes 9 and 42, and a lawyer, Roon included.

## What a rename touches

- **What people see:** the app's name (`Nib.app`, the menus, About's NIB, notifications, Settings);
  the iCloud Drive folder's name, which is `NSUbiquitousContainerName` in `Support/Info.plist` (the
  files stay where they are; iCloud caches the name, so the build number rises with it); the `nib`
  command; the icon and the menu bar mark (a pen nib: kept for Stet or Tittle, new for Spool); the
  copy deck (§5.13) and the PRD; the site, `<name>.banast.as`, which needs no new domain.
- **What stays:** the bundle ID `com.banastas.nib` and the container `iCloud.com.banastas.nib`
  (registered to the team, never shown), `Application Support/nib/`, the Xcode scheme `nib`.
- **When:** before Sparkle's feed URL and key go into a build anyone else installs (question 13's
  "When"). The two Macs' copies take the new name with one install each.
