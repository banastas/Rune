Privacy

Rune has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, ads or tracking. What you write stays in your files, on your Macs and in your iCloud Drive.

  • Your files. Rune never sends your files anywhere itself. Files in iCloud Drive (your scratch.md is in iCloud Drive › Rune) go only through Apple's iCloud Drive, under Apple's terms and privacy policy; Rune tells iCloud when to upload a version and asks it for newer ones. iCloud Drive is end-to-end encrypted only when Advanced Data Protection is on. Files outside iCloud Drive stay on your Mac.
  • Notes beside your files. So your Macs can tell which version each one has, Rune writes small notes in a hidden .nib folder beside each file it syncs in iCloud Drive: the Mac's name, the file's name, and the SHA-256 hash, size and time of the versions that Mac uploaded and loaded. Never the text. They sync through your iCloud Drive like the files, and go nowhere else.
  • Kept on your Mac only. The recovery journal, History (saved versions of your files, 30 days by default), the Sync Log and Rune's settings are kept in your Mac's Application Support folder and preferences, and are never synced or sent.
  • Updates. Once a day, Rune checks https://rune.banast.as/appcast.xml for a newer version (turn this off in Settings › General). The request includes Rune's version number, and updates are signed so only genuine ones install.
  • Import from Sublime Text reads Sublime's settings and color schemes on your Mac, changes nothing there, and sends nothing.
  • Purchases. Lemon Squeezy collects your payment and billing details as merchant of record, under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email address and order details, which we use to send receipts and to answer support requests. We don't sell or share them.
